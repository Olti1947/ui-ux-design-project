## Sample size statement (say it out loud)

"All participants were AI-simulated, with the instructor's approval: 4 interviews and 7 test sessions across 6 profiles. This shows plausible problems, not proven ones. The numbers aren't statistics."

## Self-reflection

- **Strongest evidence:** the progress-label iteration. A label test (2/4), a contextual test (0/3) and a retest (1/1) all point the same way. It also changed my own assumption: I designed the counter to reassure people, and it pressured them instead.
- **Weakest point:** every participant is simulated. A simulated participant can't surprise me the way a real person can, so the riskiest assumption (*they'll open it instead of a social app*) is still untested.
- **What I'd examine next:** the 5-day diary study with real students (HW09 next step), and whether the progress number works without the hint from day 2 onwards.
