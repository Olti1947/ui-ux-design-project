# TechTok (working title)

**Problem.** University students often have a few spare minutes: between classes, on the tram, before sleep. They want to spend them on something interesting, but the apps within thumb reach are built to keep them scrolling. A five-minute break turns into half an hour they can't remember. *(Supported by the HW02 interviews with AI-simulated participants. Not yet confirmed with real students.)*

**Target users.** University students who use their phone in short breaks and are curious about technology and general-knowledge topics.

**Idea.** A mobile reader that offers a short, finite set of hand-picked articles from about 200 quality blogs. Each card shows the title, the source, the reading time and a labelled AI summary. When the day's set is finished, the app says so and stops.

**Status.** UX/UI course project, complete through prototype v3. Research and tests used **AI-simulated participants**, with the instructor's approval ([details](docs/research/SIMULATED-PARTICIPANTS.md)). The main task was completed by 3/3 test users in under 3 minutes. The next step is a diary study with real students. The app itself is not built yet.
